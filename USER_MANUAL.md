## 1. Welcome to CashTrack

CashTrack helps you see where your money goes without sending your financial records to a cloud service.

You can use it to:

- Record expenses and income.
- Set monthly spending caps by category.
- Track bills, subscriptions, EMIs, and renewals.
- Track money you lend and money you borrow.
- Build savings pots toward targets.
- View daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly spending reports.
- Export a PDF or CSV report.
- Use built-in notes, a calculator, a stopwatch, a currency converter, world clocks, and weather.

### Local-first by design

- There is no CashTrack account and no sign-in.
- CashTrack does not connect to your bank. You enter records yourself.
- Your records are stored on your device.
- CashTrack does not sync your data to a cloud account.
- Weather and currency conversion use the internet only for those features. Your financial records are not uploaded for them.
- You are responsible for making backups before changing devices or uninstalling the app.

### Platform notes

CashTrack works on iOS, Android, and the web, but some features are native-only:

| Feature | iOS / Android | Web |
|---|---|---|
| Expense, income, budget, debt, pot, and bill tracking | Yes | Yes |
| PDF and CSV export | Yes | Yes |
| Restore from backup | Yes | No |
| App Lock | Yes | No |
| Notifications and alarms | Yes | No |
| Weather and currency rates | Yes | Yes, when online |

The best experience is the installed iOS or Android app.

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Open the app and finish onboarding

The first time you open CashTrack, the welcome walkthrough appears.

1. Read each slide.
2. Use **Next** to move forward, **Back** to return, or **Skip** to close the walkthrough.
3. Tap **Get Started** on the last slide.

You can open the walkthrough again later from **Profile & settings → Data & Privacy → Replay App Walkthrough**.

### 2.2 Choose your country and currency

During onboarding, tap **Change Country or Currency**, or use **Menu Hub → Country & Currency** at any time.

1. Search for a country or currency code.
2. Tap the country.
3. CashTrack uses that country's currency symbol and code throughout the app.

**Important:** changing the country or currency changes the currency label only. It does **not** convert your existing amounts. Choose the currency your saved amounts are already in.

### 2.3 Record your first expense

1. Tap the large **+** button in the middle of the bottom tab bar.
2. Tap **Add Expense**.
3. Choose the date.
4. Choose a category.
5. Enter the amount using the keypad. You can type a simple sum such as `12.50 + 4.75`.
6. Add an optional note.
7. Tap **Save expense**.

You can also tap **Add expense** on the Daily Cost card on the Home screen, or use the **+** button on the Transactions & Expenses screen.

### 2.4 Set your first budget

1. Open **Menu Hub**.
2. Tap **Budget Caps & Limits**.
3. Find a category, such as Food.
4. Enter a monthly amount in **Set monthly cap**.
5. Tap **Save**.

The screen shows whether each category is **Active**, **Over Limit**, or **No Limit**.

### 2.5 Add your first bill

1. Open **Menu Hub → Bills & Reminders**.
2. Tap **Add Bill**.
3. Enter the bill name and amount.
4. Choose a category and due date.
5. Choose a repeat frequency.
6. Tap **Save Reminder**.

You can also use the **+** button → **Add Bill / Reminder**.

### 2.6 Create your first savings pot

1. Tap the **+** button.
2. Tap **New Savings Pot**.
3. Choose a pot category: General, Junior, Emergency, or Milestone.
4. Enter a pot title.
5. Enter a target amount, or leave it blank for an open-ended pot.
6. Choose an allocation rule and frequency.
7. Tap **Create Savings Pot**.

### 2.7 Record money you lent or borrowed

1. Tap the **+** button.
2. Tap **Add Debt / Loan**.
3. Choose **I Lent Money** or **I Borrowed Money**.
4. Enter the person's name and the amount.
5. Choose whether the debt should affect your main balance.
6. Add a due date if needed.
7. Tap **Save Debt**.

## 4. Expenses

### 4.1 Browse and search expenses

Open the **Expenses** tab.

- The screen is titled **Transactions & Expenses**.
- Search by note, category, or amount.
- Filter by category using the chips.
- Totals update to show the filtered amount.
- Expenses are grouped by date.
- Tap an expense to open its details.

The summary card shows the total, the number of expenses, and the average per item.

### 4.2 Add an expense

Use any of these routes:

- Center **+** → **Add Expense**
- Daily Cost card → **Add expense**
- Expenses tab → **+** button
- Floating tools → **Quick Expense**

The expense form contains:

1. **Date**
2. **CATEGORY**
3. **AMOUNT / EXPRESSION**
4. **NOTE / MEMO (OPTIONAL)**
5. **RECURRING EXPENSE**

The amount keypad supports addition and subtraction. For example, `25 + 7.50 - 2` is saved as `30.50`.

If the expense would take a category over its monthly limit, a warning appears before you save.

### 4.3 Recurring expenses

Turn on **RECURRING EXPENSE** and choose:

- Weekly
- Monthly
- Yearly

How it works:

- CashTrack creates due occurrences automatically when the app opens.
- It does not create duplicates for the same period.
- Generated entries include a recurring note.
- There is no recurring end-date field. To stop a series, delete the original recurring expense.
- Deleting the original recurring expense also removes the occurrences it generated.
- Deleting one generated occurrence skips that period instead of deleting the whole series.

### 4.4 Expense details

Tap an expense to see:

- Amount
- Category
- Date
- Note
- Receipt photo, if one is already attached
- **Edit**
- **Delete**

Deleting asks for confirmation and cannot be undone.

### 4.5 Edit an expense

Tap **Edit** on the detail screen.

You can change the amount, date, category, and note. The edit form does **not** include recurring controls or receipt controls.

### 4.6 Receipts

Receipt photos are supported by the data model and appear on the detail screen if present. The current add/edit screens do not provide a button to attach, replace, or remove a receipt. Receipts already present in a restored backup will display normally.

### 4.7 Manage categories

Open **Menu Hub → Categories Manager**.

The screen is labeled **EXPENSE CATEGORIES & ICONS** and shows:

- **TOTAL CATEGORIES**
- **ACTIVE CATEGORIES**
- **BUILT-IN**
- Number of custom categories

You can:

- Add a category with a unique name and an icon from the icon pack.
- Rename custom categories.
- Delete custom categories.
- Reorder categories with the up/down arrows. The order is used in forms and reports.

Built-in categories are Food, Transport, Shopping, Bills, Health, Entertainment, and Other. Custom categories are marked **Custom**.

A category cannot be deleted while expenses still use it. Move or recategorize those expenses first.

## 5. Budgets and Spending Caps

Open **Menu Hub → Budget Caps & Limits**. The main card is labeled **SPENDING CAPS & BUDGETS**.

### 5.1 Set a category cap

1. Find the category.
2. Enter a monthly amount in **Set monthly cap**.
3. Tap **Save**.

The category then shows:

- **Active** when a cap is set and spending is within it.
- **Over Limit** when spending is above it.
- **No Limit** when no cap is set.

The top of the screen shows your **TOTAL BUDGET CAP** across all categories.

### 5.2 Remove a cap

Tap the remove button beside a saved category cap. The category returns to **No Limit**.

### 5.3 Budget warnings

When you add or edit an expense, CashTrack compares the projected category total with the cap.

- At 80% or more, it shows a heads-up.
- At 100%, it warns that the full limit will be used.
- Above 100%, it shows how far over the limit the expense would take you.

### 5.4 Budget alerts

Enable **Budget Caps** in **Profile & settings → Alerts** and allow notifications.

CashTrack can alert you when a category reaches:

- 80% of its cap
- 100% of its cap
- Over its cap

Alerts escalate only upward. Editing or deleting an expense does not trigger a lower-level alert.

### 5.5 Important behavior

Budgets are saved per category, not per calendar month. The same monthly cap is reused each month until you change or remove it. The confirmation message may mention the current month, but the cap itself persists.

### 5.6 Spending advisor

Open **Menu Hub → Spending Advisor** or use the Home screen card.

The advisor checks your current-month spending against your caps and highlights categories that are:

- Over the limit
- Close to the limit
- Spending faster than a comfortable pace

Projections apply to the current month.

## 6. Income

Open **Menu Hub → Income Streams**.

The main card shows **TOTAL MONEY IN (ALL TIME)** and the number of income records.

### 6.1 Add income

1. Enter the amount.
2. Add an optional **Source / Note**.
3. Choose the date.
4. Turn on **Recurring Income** if needed.
5. Choose Monthly, Weekly, or Yearly.
6. Tap **Add Money In**.

### 6.2 Edit or delete income

Each income entry has edit and delete buttons. Editing opens the same form and changes the button to **Update Money In**.

Deleting an income entry asks for confirmation.

### 6.3 Recurring income

Recurring income creates due entries automatically when the app opens.

- There is no recurring end-date field.
- Deleting a generated occurrence skips that period.
- Deleting the original recurring entry removes the entries it generated.

### 6.4 Auto-save from income

If you create a pot with **Auto-Save %**, CashTrack allocates that percentage of newly recorded income to the pot automatically. Allocation is capped so it never exceeds the pot target or the available income.

## 7. Savings Pots and Goals

Open **Menu Hub → Savings Goals & Pots**.

### 7.1 The pots screen

The top card shows:

- **ACTIVE SAVED**
- **ACTIVE TARGETS**

Filter tabs:

- **active**
- **completed**
- **all**

Each tab shows its count. Tap **+ New pot** to create a pot.

### 7.2 Create a pot

The screen is titled **Create Savings Pot**.

1. Choose a savings category:
   - **General** — everyday cash buffer and flexible goals
   - **Junior** — kids, education, and family futures
   - **Emergency** — a safety net for unexpected events
   - **Milestone** — vacations, vehicles, and big purchases
2. Enter a **Pot Title**.
3. Enter a **Target Amount**, or leave it blank for an open-ended pot.
4. Choose a **Savings Allocation Rule**:
   - **Auto-Save %** — save a percentage of earnings automatically
   - **Flexible Stash** — add cash anytime with no fixed rules
   - **Manual Only** — contribute only when you choose
5. If you choose Auto-Save %, set the percentage.
6. Choose a frequency: Monthly, Weekly, Daily, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, or Yearly.
7. Tap **Create Savings Pot**.

Auto-Save percentages across all active pots cannot exceed 100% of income.

### 7.3 Pot details

Tap a pot to see:

- Saved amount
- Target
- Status: **Active**, **Open-ended**, or **Completed**
- Progress percentage and bar
- Remaining amount
- Expected progress
- Ahead/behind status
- Milestones: 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100%
- **Add contribution**
- **Contribution history**
- **Edit**
- **Delete Pot / Goal**

### 7.4 Add a contribution

1. Enter an amount.
2. Tap **Contribute**.

If the contribution reaches the target, the pot is marked complete.

Contributions are tracked inside the pot. They are not automatically recorded as an expense or deducted from your main balance.

### 7.5 Edit or delete a pot

- **Edit** lets you change the pot's details and allocation.
- **Delete Pot / Goal** permanently removes the pot and its contribution history.
- Individual contributions can be deleted from the history.

## 8. Debts and IOUs

Open **Menu Hub → Debts & IOUs**.

### 8.1 The debts screen

The summary shows:

- **YOU ARE OWED**
- **YOU OWE**
- **Net Position** — credit or debit

Filter tabs:

- **Active**
- **All**
- **Settled**
- **Lent**
- **Borrowed**

### 8.2 Add a debt

1. Choose **I Lent Money** or **I Borrowed Money**.
2. Enter the **PERSON NAME**.
3. Enter the **AMOUNT**.
4. Choose whether it should **Affect main balance**.
   - Lent money can be logged as an expense.
   - Borrowed money can be logged as income.
5. Choose the **TRANSACTION DATE**.
6. Optionally enable a **DUE DATE**.
7. Add an optional **NOTE**.
8. Tap **Save Debt**.

### 8.3 Debt details

Tap a debt to see:

- Original total
- Amount paid
- Remaining balance
- Status: **Active**, **Paid Off**, or **Settled**
- Due date
- Note
- Payment history
- **Record Partial Payment**
- **Settle Full**
- **Reminder**
- **Delete**

### 8.4 Partial payments and settlement

- **Record Partial Payment** tracks a payment against the debt.
- Partial payments are not automatically added to the expense ledger.
- **Settle Full** marks the entire debt as settled.
- Settling does not create an expense or income entry. Use **Affect main balance** when creating the debt if you want the original transaction reflected in your ledger.
- **Reminder** opens the system share sheet with a prepared message.

Deleting a debt record cannot be undone.

## 9. Bills and Reminders

Open **Menu Hub → Bills & Reminders**.

### 9.1 The bills screen

The top card shows:

- **TOTAL DUE** for the current month
- Overdue count
- Due-today count
- **All Clear** when nothing is outstanding

Filter tabs:

- **All**
- **Due Today**
- **Overdue**
- **Upcoming**
- **Paid**

### 9.2 Add a bill

1. Tap **Add Bill**.
2. Enter the **BILL / REMINDER NAME**.
3. Enter the **AMOUNT**, or turn on **Amount varies**.
4. Choose a **CATEGORY**:
   - Bills
   - EMI
   - Loan
   - Rent
   - Credit Card
   - Insurance
   - Subscription
   - Education
   - Tax
   - Utilities
   - Other
5. If you choose EMI, optionally enter the total loan amount and number of installments.
6. Choose the **DUE DATE**.
7. Optionally enable **High-Priority Alarm Mode** and set an exact alarm time.
8. Choose the **REPEAT FREQUENCY**: One-time, Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly.
9. Tap **Save Reminder**.

### 9.3 Mark a bill as paid

1. Tap **Mark Paid** on the bill, or open it from the list.
2. Confirm the **PAYMENT AMOUNT**.
3. Choose the **PAYMENT DATE**.
4. Leave **Record as an expense** on if you want the payment added to your expense ledger and budgets.
5. Add an optional note.
6. Tap **Confirm Payment**.

For a recurring bill, a full payment advances the bill to its next due date. For a one-time bill, marking it paid closes it.

### 9.4 Skip a recurring bill

Recurring bills have a **Skip** action. Skipping advances the bill to its next cycle without recording an expense.

### 9.5 Delete a bill

Deleting a reminder asks for confirmation and removes the bill and its scheduled notifications.

### 9.6 Bill calendar

Open **Menu Hub → Bill & Renewal Calendar**, or tap the calendar banner on the Bills screen.

The **Renewal Calendar** shows a monthly grid with bills on their due dates.

- Use the arrows to change month.
- Tap a day to see its bills.
- Tap a bill to open its payment screen.

The calendar always uses Sunday as the first day of the week, regardless of the **First Day of Week** preference.

### 9.7 Subscriptions and recurring hub

Open **Menu Hub → Subscriptions & Recurring Hub**, or tap the link on the Home screen.

This screen shows recurring bills and subscriptions with:

- **MONTHLY OUTFLOW**
- **ACTIVE RENEWALS**
- Renewal countdowns
- **Add recurring bill**

The monthly outflow converts weekly, monthly, and yearly bills to a monthly estimate.

## 13. App Lock

Open **Profile & settings → Preferences → App Lock**.

### 13.1 Turn on App Lock

1. Make sure your device has a screen lock, Face ID, or fingerprint set up.
2. Turn on **Require device authentication**.
3. Authenticate with your device security when prompted.

When App Lock is on:

- CashTrack asks for device authentication when it starts.
- It locks again after 30 seconds in the background.
- It uses Face ID, fingerprint, or your existing device passcode.
- CashTrack does **not** create or store a separate PIN.

### 13.2 Test the lock

When App Lock is on, the **Check it works** card appears.

Tap **Lock now** to lock the app immediately.

### 13.3 If device security is unavailable

If your device has no screen lock, App Lock cannot be enabled. The app explains that you need to set up device security first.

If you remove your device screen lock later, you can turn App Lock off from the lock screen so you are not trapped.

### 13.4 Web

App Lock is available in the iOS and Android app only. The web version reports that it is unsupported.

## 14. Backup, Restore, and Data

CashTrack is local-only. There is no automatic cloud backup. Make a backup before:

- Changing phones
- Uninstalling the app
- Clearing app data
- Resetting the device
- Making large edits you might want to undo

### 14.1 Export expenses as CSV

**Profile & settings → Data & Privacy → Export CSV Spreadsheet**

- Exports all recorded expenses.
- Columns: **Date, Category, Amount, Currency, Memo**.
- On the web, the file downloads.
- On iOS/Android, the share/save sheet opens.

CSV is for spreadsheets and analysis. It does not include income, pots, debts, bills, notes, or settings. Use a full backup for those.

### 14.2 Create a full backup

**Profile & settings → Data & Privacy → Back Up All Data**

The backup is a JSON file named like `CashTrack_Backup_YYYY-MM-DD.json`.

It includes:

- Expenses
- Preferences and profile settings
- Notes
- Debts and payments
- Income
- Pots, goals, and contributions
- Bills and reminders
- Recurring skip records
- Photos and receipts, where available

Save the file somewhere safe.

**Security note:** backup files are plain JSON and are not encrypted or password-protected. Treat them like sensitive financial documents.

### 14.3 Restore a backup

**Profile & settings → Data & Privacy → Restore From Backup**

1. Choose a CashTrack backup file.
2. Review the preview: expenses, income, debts, pots, bills, and notes.
3. Tap **Replace All Data** to confirm.

Restore **replaces everything currently on the device** with the backup. It cannot be undone.

- Restore is available in the iOS and Android app only.
- On the web, CashTrack explains that restore is available in the native app.
- If a restore fails partway through, your previous data may be incomplete. Keep a recent backup and try again.

### 14.4 Replay the walkthrough

**Profile & settings → Data & Privacy → Replay App Walkthrough**

This opens the onboarding tour again. It does not delete your data.

### 14.5 Wipe all local data

**Profile & settings → Data & Privacy → Danger Zone → Wipe All Local Data**

CashTrack asks twice:

1. **Reset All Local Data?**
2. **Final Confirmation**

Tap **Wipe Everything** on the final confirmation to permanently delete all transactions, notes, goals, debts, and settings, and reset the app to defaults.

This cannot be undone. Make a backup first if you might need the data.

## 15. Notifications

Notifications are available on iOS and Android. The web version does not send notifications.

### 15.1 Bill reminders

When **Bill Reminders** is enabled and notifications are allowed, CashTrack schedules:

- A reminder **3 days before** the due date at 9:00 AM
- A reminder **1 day before** the due date at 9:00 AM
- A reminder **on the due date** at 9:00 AM

If **High-Priority Alarm Mode** is on and an exact alarm time is set, the due-date notification uses that time instead.

### 15.2 Budget alerts

When **Budget Caps** is enabled and notifications are allowed, CashTrack can alert you at 80%, 100%, and over-limit.

### 15.3 Daily check-in and motivation

When enabled, CashTrack sends:

- **Daily Check-in** — a daily reminder to review your money
- **Daily Motivation** — a daily financial quote notification

### 15.4 Test alarm

**Profile & settings → Alerts → Test Alarm Sound**

CashTrack schedules a test notification after 5 seconds.

- Turn off Silent mode to hear it.
- On Android, the high-priority alarm channel uses maximum importance and vibration.
- If notifications are off, the test does not schedule.

### 15.5 Notification limitations

- CashTrack uses the system notification sound. It does not include a custom ringtone.
- It cannot loop like a phone alarm.
- Silent mode, Do Not Disturb, and device battery restrictions can block sound or delivery.
- In Expo Go, notification behavior may differ from a standalone build. Use a development or production build to test alarms reliably.
- Android exact alarms may require the **SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM** permission and appropriate device settings.

### 15.6 Managing permissions

If notifications do not arrive:

1. Open your device settings.
2. Find CashTrack.
3. Allow notifications, sound, and alarms.
4. Check Silent mode and Do Not Disturb.
5. Return to **Profile & settings → Alerts** and use **Test Alarm Sound**.

## 17. Troubleshooting and FAQ

### The app shows sample weather

Live weather needs location permission and a network connection.

1. Allow location access for CashTrack.
2. Pull down on the Weather screen to retry.
3. If weather still fails, sample data is shown with an offline banner.

### Currency rates look old or say offline

The converter uses live rates when available and falls back to cached or offline rates.

1. Check your internet connection.
2. Reopen the converter.
3. Use the refresh action if shown.

### Notifications are not arriving

- Allow notifications for CashTrack in device settings.
- Turn off Silent mode and Do Not Disturb.
- Use **Test Alarm Sound** in Profile → Alerts.
- Test in a development or production build rather than Expo Go.
- On Android, check exact-alarm permission and battery optimization.

### I cannot restore a backup on the web

Restore is available in the iOS and Android app only. Open the same backup file in the native app.

### I cannot enable App Lock

Your device needs a screen lock, Face ID, or fingerprint. Set up device security first. App Lock is not available on the web.

### My PDF opened a print dialog instead of downloading

On the web, CashTrack opens a print-ready tab. Choose **Save as PDF** in the browser print dialog.

### My CSV did not download on mobile

On iOS/Android, the CSV opens the system share sheet. Choose **Save to Files**, **Download**, or your preferred destination.

### My recurring expense or income did not appear

Recurring entries are generated when their due period is reached and the app is opened. Check the original entry's start date and frequency.

### My budget cap is still there next month

This is expected. Budget caps are saved per category and reused each month until you change or remove them.

### The bill calendar starts on Sunday

This is expected. The bill calendar is always Sunday-first, even if **First Day of Week** is set to Monday.

### I cannot find an expense

- Clear the search box.
- Set the category filter to **All**.
- Check the date grouping.
- Check whether the expense was deleted.
- If you restored a backup, confirm the restore completed.

### I deleted something by mistake

Most deletions cannot be undone. Restore a backup if you have one. This is why regular backups are recommended.

### The app feels slow with many records

- Close and reopen the app.
- Keep the app updated.
- Use search and category filters instead of scrolling long lists.
- On older devices, large receipt photos can increase backup size.

### I changed my phone. How do I move my data?

1. On the old phone, create a full backup.
2. Save the JSON file somewhere you can access from the new phone.
3. Install CashTrack on the new phone.
4. Use **Restore From Backup**.
5. Confirm **Replace All Data**.

## 18. Current Limitations

These are known behaviors in CashTrack 1.0.0. They are documented so you can plan around them.

### Branding

Most of the app uses **CashTrack**. The About screen and logo use **Spendly**. They are the same app.

### Budget caps are not month-specific

A category cap is a single persistent monthly limit. Setting a new cap replaces the old one for all future months. CashTrack does not keep a separate cap history per month.

### Receipt attachment UI is missing

Receipt photos can be stored and displayed, but the current add/edit screens do not provide a button to attach, replace, or remove a receipt.

### Bill calendar is Sunday-first

The bill calendar ignores the **First Day of Week** preference and always starts on Sunday.

### Period report exports are limited

- Daily reports: PDF only.
- Monthly reports: PDF and CSV.
- Weekly and yearly period reports: on-screen only, no export button.
- Profile CSV: all expenses, not a full data backup.

### App Lock has no separate PIN

App Lock relies entirely on the device's screen lock, Face ID, fingerprint, or passcode. CashTrack does not create or store its own PIN.

### Restore is native-only

Restore from backup is not available on the web. Backups can still be created and exported on the web.

### Restore is not transactional

If a restore fails partway through, the device may be left with incomplete data. Keep a recent backup and retry.

### Notification sound is system-controlled

CashTrack does not include a custom ringtone and cannot loop a notification like a phone alarm. Silent mode, Do Not Disturb, and battery restrictions can suppress it.

### Web limitations

The web version does not support App Lock, restore, or notifications. Some share-sheet actions become browser downloads or print dialogs.

### Stopwatch state is not persistent

The stopwatch resets when the app is reloaded.

### Currency changes do not convert amounts

Changing the country or currency changes the display label only. Existing amounts are not converted using exchange rates.

### Recurring entries have no end date

Recurring expenses and income repeat until you delete the original entry. There is no end-date field in the form.

### Debt settlement does not create a ledger entry

Partial payments and full settlement are tracked on the debt itself. They are not automatically added to expenses or income. Use **Affect main balance** when creating the debt if you want the original transaction in your ledger.

### Pot contributions are not expenses

Contributions are tracked inside the pot. They do not automatically reduce your main balance or appear in the expense ledger.
